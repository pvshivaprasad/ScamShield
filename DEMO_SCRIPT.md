# ScamShield — three-minute demo script

## 0:00–0:20 — The problem
“Scam messages often rely on urgency and trust. ScamShield doesn't just return a label: it explains the warning signs and offers a safer next step.”

## 0:20–1:10 — Fake KYC
Select the **Fake KYC** example. Point to the risk score, highlighted phrases, account-block threat, brand/link mismatch, and urgency. Emphasise that the score is a risk assessment, not a guarantee.

## 1:10–1:40 — A legitimate safety warning
Select **Real bank OTP**. Explain why a message telling a user never to share an OTP is different from a message requesting one.

## 1:40–2:20 — Bulk review
Open **Scan many**, upload a CSV with a `text` column, filter by risk, and export the results.

## 2:20–2:45 — Explainability and evidence
Open **Accuracy** and describe the model-plus-rules design. Clearly state that India-style transfer examples are synthetic and independent real-world validation is still needed.

## 2:45–3:00 — Safety action
“Verify financial requests using the official bank app or website. If money has been lost to cyber fraud in India, call 1930 and report at cybercrime.gov.in.”

## Before recording
- Use only synthetic/example messages.
- Never show real OTPs, passwords, bank account details, or victim data.
- Confirm the local app and all demonstration screens work.
- Do not claim perfect detection or production-grade accuracy.
